Staying below ISR/2 makes it *arithmetically impossible* for two step pulses to
land on consecutive ticks -- which is what guarantees the DRV8825 its 1.9 us
minimum LOW time. This script **refuses** to emit a config that breaks it.

> ### :warning: CONFIRM THE 100 mm MIDDLE GAP
> This is fine **only if your suction inlet sits there** (side brushes sweeping
> debris inward is the standard design). If there is nothing in the middle, you
> have a 100 mm uncleaned stripe down the centre of **every lane**, and
> no path planner can fix it.

> ### :warning: THE 9 cm WALL STRIP -- a mechanical limit, not a software one
> The robot centre can never get closer than **0.240 m** to a wall
> (the wheels are the widest part). The brush reaches 0.150 m beyond
> that -- so it stops **9 cm short of every wall**.
>
> Options: accept it; reduce `safety_margin` 0.03 -> 0.01 (strip becomes
> 7 cm); or mount the brushes further outboard.
